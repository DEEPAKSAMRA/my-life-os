Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

My Life OS is a personal Android app made by Deepak Kumar Meena for his own and his family's use. It is not sold, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

1. Where your data is kept

Everything the app stores — tasks, notes, reminders, lists, settings and anything shown from connected accounts — is kept only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There is no My Life OS server, and the app does not upload your data anywhere. Android backup of the app's data is turned off.

2. Google user data

Only if you choose to connect a Google account, the app asks Google for specific read-only permissions through Google's own sign-in screen. Today that is:

  • Your email address (userinfo.email, openid), used only to label the connected account in the app.
  • Read-only Google Calendar (calendar.readonly), used to check that access keeps working and, once that feature is turned on, to show your upcoming events on your phone.

Further read-only permissions (for example Gmail, Tasks or Drive) are asked for only if you turn on that feature, and this policy will list them before they are used.

The app reads Google data directly from Google to your phone. It never changes, deletes or sends anything in your Google account. Google sign-in tokens are held by Google Play services on your phone; the app does not store them.

Limited Use. My Life OS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it.

3. Other services the app contacts

  • Open-Meteo (weather, air quality, city search): only the chosen city's coordinates, or the city name you type when searching.
  • News feeds you choose: only the feed address is requested.
  • CricketData (optional, test only): only when you tap Fetch, using your own API key.

These services receive the normal technical details of any internet request, such as your IP address, under their own policies. The app sends them nothing else about you.

4. Permissions on the phone

Internet access; notifications (asked when you first set a reminder); exact alarms (only if you switch it on); and restoring reminders after a restart. The app does not use your location, contacts, microphone, camera or files. Voice input uses Android's own speech screen.

5. Deleting your data and withdrawing access

  • Disconnecting a Google account in the app deletes its data from the phone and withdraws the app's access at Google.
  • Settings → Data controls → Delete all local data removes everything the app stored and withdraws Google access for every connected account.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the phone.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

6. Children

The app is for the developer's own family and is not directed at children.

7. Changes

If what the app accesses changes, this page is updated before the change is used, with a new date above.
