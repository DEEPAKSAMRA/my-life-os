My Life OS

A private, personal Android dashboard for one family's own use. It is not sold, has no ads and is not on the Play Store.

What it does

  • Tasks, reminders, grocery lists and notes, stored only on the phone.
  • Weather, air quality, news headlines and a Panchang for a chosen city.
  • With your permission, read-only access to your own Google account (for example your calendar), shown on your phone.

How it treats your data

  • Your data stays on your phone. There is no My Life OS server.
  • Google data is only read, never changed, sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • You can disconnect any account in the app, which also withdraws its access at Google.
